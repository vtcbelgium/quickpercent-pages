Mathematical Formula & Calculation Steps

The standard formula for calculating percentage difference between two numbers is:

Percentage Difference = ((V2 - V1) / |V1|) × 100%
  • Subtract the initial value from the final value: Δ = V2 - V1
  • Divide the difference by the absolute value of the initial value: Δ / |V1|
  • Multiply by 100 to convert into percentage format.
