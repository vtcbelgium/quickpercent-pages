Frequently Asked Questions

How is "Percentage of a number" calculated?

A percentage is a fraction out of 100. To find X% of Y, the formula is (X ÷ 100) × Y. For example, 15% of 200 = (15 ÷ 100) × 200 = 30.

How does "Percentage change" work?

Percentage change compares a starting value to an ending value using ((New − Original) ÷ Original) × 100. If the result is positive, it represents an increase; if negative, a decrease. For example, moving from 80 to 100 is (100 − 80) ÷ 80 × 100 = +25%.

How are discounts and sales tax combined?

The discount percentage is applied first to find the discounted subtotal: Price × (1 − Discount ÷ 100). If an optional tax or VAT percentage is specified, it is applied directly to that discounted subtotal: Subtotal × (1 + Tax ÷ 100). Savings are displayed before tax.

Can I use decimal commas (European format)?

Yes. QuickPercent accepts both standard period decimals (12.5) and European comma decimals (12,5). We automatically normalize them before calculation.
