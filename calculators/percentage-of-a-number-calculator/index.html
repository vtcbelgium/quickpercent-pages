Frequently Asked Questions

How do you find a percentage of a number without a calculator?

Convert the percentage to a decimal by dividing by 100 (e.g. 15% = 0.15) and multiply by the target number (0.15 × 250 = 37.5).

Can I calculate percentages greater than 100%?

Yes! For example, 150% of 80 is (1.50 × 80) = 120. QuickPercent supports values up to ±1 trillion.
