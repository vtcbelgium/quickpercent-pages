Mathematical Formula

Tax Amount = Pre-Tax Price × (Tax Rate % / 100) | Final Total = Pre-Tax Price + Tax Amount

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate sales tax backward from a total price?

To find the pre-tax price from a tax-inclusive total, divide the total by (1 + Tax Rate / 100). For example, $108 with 8% tax: $108 / 1.08 = $100.
