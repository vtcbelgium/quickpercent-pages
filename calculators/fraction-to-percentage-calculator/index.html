Mathematical Formula

Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100%

Step-by-Step Example

A score of 18 correct answers out of 24 questions: (18 / 24) × 100% = 75.0%.

Common Use Cases

  • Calculating test scores and academic grading percentages
  • Survey results (e.g., 42 out of 60 respondents agreed = 70%)
  • Project completion and milestone tracking
  • Polls, voter turnout, and sample statistical proportions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you turn a fraction into a percentage?

Divide the numerator (top number) by the denominator (bottom number) to get a decimal, then multiply by 100 to get the percentage.

What happens if the denominator is 0?

Division by zero is mathematically undefined. QuickPercent will prompt you to enter a nonzero total.
