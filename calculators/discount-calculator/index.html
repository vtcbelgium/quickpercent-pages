Mathematical Formula

Savings = Original Price × (Discount % / 100) | Final Price = (Original Price - Savings) × (1 + Tax % / 100)

Step-by-Step Example

An $80 jacket with a 25% discount saves $20.00, resulting in a sale price of $60.00 (or $64.80 with 8% sales tax).

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a 30% discount in my head?

Find 10% of the price by moving the decimal point one place to the left, multiply by 3, and subtract that amount from the original price.

Does this calculator support local sales tax?

Yes! Enter your city or state sales tax percentage to see both the discounted subtotal and the final out-of-pocket register total.
